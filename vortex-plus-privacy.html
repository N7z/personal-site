Vortex Plus - Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 August 2026

What is collected

Nothing. Vortex Plus does not collect, transmit, sell or share any user data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no remote servers and no third party services.

What is stored, and where

Two things, both kept by the browser through the chrome.storage API:

  • Feature toggles - which features you turned on or off, in chrome.storage.sync, so they follow your Chrome profile.
  • Saved looks - the outfits you explicitly choose to save, in chrome.storage.local, on your device only.

Neither ever leaves your browser. Uninstalling the extension removes both.

Network requests

Vortex Plus runs only on playvortex.io and only talks to playvortex.io, using the session you are already signed in with. It reads pages such as the catalog and player profiles to display its own UI, and it changes your outfit when you ask it to, for example by pressing "Copy outfit" or saving a look. No request is made to any other domain.

Permissions

  • storage - to keep the two items listed above.
  • Host access to playvortex.io and its subdomains - to add the interface and to call the site's own endpoints on your behalf.
