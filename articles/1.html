How I survived php2shell

created at: 2026-10-22

> be any php developer
> wake up
> check phone
> panic

I am in charge of maintaining ~12 servers with just about 500 clients, at least half of them running on deprecated PHP versions from CloudLinux's PHP packages.

When I heard about the CVEs, the first thing I did was check if there was already a patch (there wasn't, until 11am GMT-3). Then I proceeded to check if my firewall provider, Imunify360, had shipped any WAF rules against it. Surprisingly, they had! I updated the firewall and some parameters in php.ini, restarted everything and hoped for the best.

Then 11am came and I checked for the new packages... there were none. The only quick releases were for PHP 8.2+ by the official team, and the backports by Surý were no help to me on another provider.

I spent the entire day keeping an eye out for exploitation attempts, and the number at the end of the day was higher than I expected: 40+ attempts on different websites, 100% of which were blocked by Imunify before even reaching PHP.

Later that night CloudLinux released backports for all versions from 5.6 onwards. That still sent me a very clear message: we NEED to upgrade our websites. PHP 5, 7 and 8.1 are all in the past and need to go extinct. Every day that passes is a new risk waiting to be discovered, and with AI and machine learning, that risk is only going to increase.

That's why I made the decision to actually deprecate EOL PHP versions: no new websites are accepted on them, and old ones are being prompted to migrate to newer versions. It makes me scared to think about how many systems out there are not on patched PHP versions, and never will be.
